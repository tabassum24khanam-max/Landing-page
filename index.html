    01 How it works

    Three agents. One judge.
    Zero indicator scripts.

    No single model guesses. Three specialists analyze independently, a fourth weighs their arguments and rules, and a mechanical layer guards every position in between.

    01 · THE MATH

    Chief Market Analyst

    Reads 35+ indicators — trend, momentum, volume, volatility, market structure, Monte Carlo simulation. Forms its own view first, then must state the strongest argument against itself.

    • Inputs35+ indicators
    • Required outputcounter-argument

    02 · THE NEWS

    Intelligence Chief

    Pulls 10+ sources, then triages: finds the single dominant catalyst and judges whether it's already priced in — only surprises move markets. Also watches insider filings, options flow and dark pool activity.

    • Sources10+ feeds
    • Judgespriced in / not

    03 · THE JUDGE

    Head of Trading Desk

    Weighs both reports plus a risk officer's neutral notes — earnings dates, correlated positions. Can overrule any agent with a stated reason, and votes three independent times, taking the majority.

    • Voting3 runs, majority
    • Memoryown track record

    Between decisions — mechanical, always on

    The risk layer

    Protects every open position continuously. Costs nothing to run and needs no AI call.

    Trailing stop
    follows the peak, exits on real reversal
    Breakeven stop
    once meaningfully green, can't go red
    Anti-wick-out
    stop must stay breached ~1 min, no shake-outs
    Time-decaying stop
    losing positions get progressively tighter
    Thesis-flip exit
    AI reverses with conviction → position flips
    News trailing stop
    adverse breaking news tightens the stop
    Correlation veto
    blocks stacking the same bet twice
    Chop detector
    shrinks or skips directionless markets

    The Sentry

    checks every 10s · near-zero cost

    A lightweight watcher on every open position. It ignores normal noise and bounce-backs, and only wakes the expensive AI early for a real straight-line move, a fresh high-impact headline, or a new SEC 8-K filing. Hard-capped so it can never spam the system.

    Dual-brain mode

    V2 vs V3 · side by side

    Two decision pipelines run at once on separate paper accounts, scored in real time. Strategy changes get validated against a live control, not guessed at.

    Built to be checked, not believed

    The audience for a trading bot is skeptical, correctly. Four features exist purely to make the system checkable.

    Real equity vs. headline equity

    Most bots flatter their number by banking winners and quietly holding losers off-screen. ULTRAMAX shows both, always: the headline figure, and the true mark-to-market value including every open loss.

    Forensic log

    Every trade is recorded with a full reasoning autopsy: what each agent said, what the judge ruled, and why. Fully exportable.

    agent ANALYST → bull, conf. 0.71

    agent INTEL → not priced in

    judge ruling: LONG (2/3 vote)

    AI-dead guard

    If the AI services fail, the system refuses to trade rather than guessing, and shows a clear offline warning until service is restored.

    Honest model accuracy

    The statistical model reports its real out-of-sample accuracy and is treated as one weak input — never as an oracle the others defer to.
