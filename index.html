POSITION LIVE · GUARDED

BOBCAT is a pipeline. Signals from market data, newswires and the calendar are read in parallel by three independent reasoners — a quantitative read of price structure, a context read of what is already priced in, and a counter-case that argues against the view. A separate judge weighs all three against a neutral risk picture, can overrule any of them, and is cross-checked before anything is risked. A mechanical risk kernel then holds the position continuously, with or without the AI.

02 How it works

It reasons. It doesn't just react.
